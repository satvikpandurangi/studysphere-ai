# Private institutional launch direction

## Current owner revision — 29 October 2026

The first school partnership is **free**. Neither the school nor founder can invest in database, hosting, email or AI. The target is **1,500 simultaneously active students**, Standards 1–10, with CBSE and Karnataka State Board (KSEEB). The real school identity, approved materials and email/domain arrangements remain open. One provisional workspace is active: Vidyanidhi Public School, Dharwad, Karnataka. The owner selected one school only; additional schools are deferred. This supersedes the first-customer funding prerequisite below; the 22 October text is retained as historical rationale. Private verified access, existing design, one reusable codebase and learning without AI remain accepted. No paid service is authorized. Capacity is a target, not a verified capability; apply the [first-school release gates](../launch/first-school-readiness.md). Later paid-school sales remain a future business direction.

## Historical decision — 22 October 2026

Decision recorded **22 October 2026**, following the owner's clarification after the competitive research. This changes delivery priorities, not implemented capabilities.

## Accepted business direction

Start with specific schools or institutions that fund an agreed setup and its running costs. Deliver a private workspace customized for that institution and its authorized users, using its approved learning materials. The founder has no budget to subsidize public hosting, database or AI usage. Do not incur paid service costs in anticipation of future customers.

An open public learning platform and individual subscriptions are deferred. They remain possible future directions, subject to demand and sustainable funding. Standards 11–12 / PUC 1–2 and colleges remain planned scope; only the stages actually implemented and accepted for a customer may be offered.

## First delivery boundary

Deliver a responsive web application first, retaining the existing platform design and shared components. PWA installation follows core workflows. Dedicated Android, iOS and desktop applications are deferred until a funded customer requirement justifies them. Installation does not imply offline capability.

The first engagement should name one institution, its actual learner cohort, subjects and approved materials, responsible staff, required workflows and acceptance evidence. A school, PUC institution or college does not automatically imply support for every education stage.

Institution-only access must come from verified membership/enrollment and content entitlements. A typed school label is not proof. Do not restore the removed school approval queue. Preserve existing Standard 1–5 guardian and Standard 6–10 independent account behavior while designing enrollment; do not invent policy for older learners.

The core experience must work without an AI request: staff publish approved material, authorized learners study and practice, progress persists, and teachers can take useful action. AI is optional and requires an explicitly funded allowance before being enabled for customer use. Current source supports an optional tutor path; a complete institution-specific feature switch and budget-control workflow are not yet implemented.

## Delivery recommendations

These are working recommendations to scope with the first customer, not claims that the system already supports them:

- Maintain one reusable codebase. Customize identity/branding, curriculum, materials, permitted features and agreed workflows through configuration where practical. Price and assess deeper customization separately; avoid permanent customer-specific forks.
- Decide shared versus dedicated deployment after reviewing the institution's isolation, recovery and operating requirements. A separately branded UI is not evidence of data isolation.
- Prefer institution-controlled provider accounts where workable. Otherwise explicitly assign account ownership, payment responsibility, access, backups and handover. No deployment/account ownership model has been selected yet.
- Agree setup funding before provisioning paid services or importing a production cohort. Account for content onboarding, branding, verification, training and acceptance work separately from recurring operation and support. Amounts and payment terms remain undecided.
- Begin with a bounded cohort and budget. Document who approves increases, what happens at a limit and how service/support continues or ends. Automatic checkout and individual subscription billing are not prerequisites for an agreed manually administered institutional pilot.

## Cost rules

The institution's funded operating allowance must account for database, storage/transfer, hosting/API, email, backup/recovery and support, plus AI only if requested. Do not promise unlimited usage or treat a free provider tier as a permanent production guarantee. Existing [pilot limits](../operations/pilot-budget.md) remain unchanged until a funded implementation deliberately changes them.

To reduce AI demand, reuse teacher-approved material and deterministic practice/revision first. Teacher-reviewed reusable study aids may be investigated later; generation, storage, source rights and correction still have costs. Per-student and per-institution controls, monitoring and a graceful AI-unavailable experience need verification before enabling a paid allowance. Never expose provider secrets to learners.

## Product distinction

Keep the research conclusions: personal productivity overlaps with the supplied StudySphere; curriculum/PUC workspaces such as StudentPal are closer comparisons; regional languages, adaptive quizzes, parent reports and teacher views already exist; institutional value needs more than publishing and activity counts.

Compete initially on dependable delivery for a specific institution: its approved materials, controlled access, a usable teacher-to-learner workflow and support within the funded budget. Teacher-reviewed intervention, source-correction follow-up and bilingual diagnosis remain **research candidates**, not approved implementation commitments or market-first claims. See the [competitive report](../research/2026-10-22-competitive-landscape.md).

## Before the next implementation is scoped

Record the first institution/cohort, the minimum learning and teacher workflows, branding needs, content ownership/approval, account and enrollment policy, deployment ownership, funded limits and support responsibilities. Then select the smallest deliverable and acceptance tests from the [roadmap](roadmap.md). No customer, commercial quote, service purchase or deployment is established by this decision.
