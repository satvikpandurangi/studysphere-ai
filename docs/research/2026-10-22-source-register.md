# Competitive research source register

Checked 22 October 2026. Companion to the [research report](2026-10-22-competitive-landscape.md). These are research sources, not product endorsements or independently certified capability claims.

## How to interpret the evidence

**Browser:** visible public interface was inspected. **Official page:** readable vendor/help/documentation text was retrieved. **Search capture:** official publisher/vendor material was visible in search results, but full page retrieval was limited or was not repeated. **Announcement:** dated vendor release, with rollout restrictions preserved.

All external features remain vendor claims unless a specific public interface observation says otherwise. No competitor's authenticated learning, security, payment, app-install or administrative flows were tested. “Not found in reviewed pages” must never be rewritten as “not offered anywhere.”

## Targeted novelty checks

These are query themes, not proof of an exhaustive novelty search:

| Candidate idea | Query examples | What challenged it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local PUC/Kannada tutor | `"PUC" "Kannada" "AI tutor"` | StudentPal, regional Biology content and state-board tutoring pages |
| Source changes updating study items | `"learning" "source version" "flashcards" updates`; `"syllabus" "diff" "flashcards"` | Honen maintenance, Revu history/review and other emerging projects |
| Error diagnosis and specific repair | `"study platform" "misconception" "language" "diagnose"` | Established adaptive systems, newer error-type products and niche exam products |
| Paper-assisted personalized learning | `site:help.wayground.com "Paper Mode"` | Documented response-card workflow; separate vendor evidence for handwritten papers |
| Evidence carried across courses | `site:docs.moodle.org learning plans competencies` | Existing competency evidence and review |
| Mistakes becoming a plan without mandatory AI | `"syllabus" "correction" "flashcards" learning platform` | Study Sheet's explicit data-first revision proposition |

The narrow differentiation candidates in the report survived only as **combinations or quality improvements not settled by reviewed material**, not globally unprecedented features. Patent searching, app-store completeness and every regional language market were outside scope.

## Contradictions and interpretation safeguards

1. **StudySphere platforms:** broad homepage wording includes Windows, but the specific Windows card says coming soon. Report the narrower availability statement. An Android ZIP link is not proof of a store-published, working app.
2. **Knowt packaging:** older teacher-help content and the current pricing/newer free-plan explanation differ. The report uses the current annual total and states the conflict rather than combining both offers.
3. **EMBIBE prices:** listing body and developer response differ. Exact checkout pricing remains unresolved; one user's billing complaint is anecdotal.
4. **Higher education marketing:** StudentPal mentions this stage, but the inspected catalogue did not establish a current degree/semester course offering. Public PUC listings were directly visible.
5. **Google/Canvas releases:** dates and rollout/feature-option constraints matter. An announcement does not guarantee availability to every Indian account or school.
6. **CourseLogic comparisons:** its claims about competitors are marketing, not authority. In particular, dismissing NotebookLM as only Q&A conflicts with Google's own documented study tools. Use each vendor's own current documentation.
7. **DIKSHA documentation:** the official access page and older/versioned help can differ on OS requirements and desktop availability. The report relies on mobile/web/offline principles, not a promised current desktop build.
8. **Safety and efficacy claims:** vendor “compliant,” “bias-free,” “cheat-proof,” score-improvement, user-count and review-rating statements were not independently audited. No such claims are adopted for our product.
9. **Regional breadth:** saying a product lists a board or language is not evidence of complete, accurate coverage for every subject/stream/year.
10. **Non-results:** a blocked fetch, login wall or absent keyword search does not establish that a product lacks a capability.

## Remaining research before commitment

- Obtain authorized hands-on access to the closest two or three competitors and compare the same teacher/learner tasks.
- Validate source citation correctness, subject coverage, Kannada terminology and exam-answer quality using educator-reviewed samples.
- Check current app distribution and offline behavior on actual target devices.
- Ask institution buyers about procurement, existing LMS constraints, content ownership, support and operating budget.
- Validate the recommended problem with users and compare outcomes, not only feature counts.
- Conduct separate brand/naming research; this register does not establish legal rights.

No application code or approved product scope was changed during this research.
